Defence in depth

Every layer is a decision.

Perimeter, identity, application, data. A weakness in one is survivable; a weakness in all four is not. We test each layer separately, then test what happens when they fail together.

01

Perimeter

Exposed services, DNS, TLS, edge configuration and cloud boundaries.

02

Identity

Authentication flows, session handling, privilege boundaries and MFA gaps.

03

Application

Business logic, injection classes, authorisation flaws and dependency risk.

04

Data

Storage, encryption, retention, backups and what happens after a breach.

Questions

Before you write to us.

Always. We do not test anything without a signed scope that names the assets, the time window, the contacts and the legal boundaries. If a client cannot produce that, we stop.

NDAs are welcome and mutual. Evidence is encrypted at rest, retained for the agreed period, then destroyed. Access is limited to the named people on the engagement.

Only with written agreement, and only after a fix exists. Client findings stay private by default. Independent research on third-party products follows coordinated disclosure, usually ninety days.

Most work is remote. Physical and wireless assessments, or anything that needs hardware access, are planned on-site with enough lead time for travel and logistics.

One call to understand the system, one short scoping document, then a two to six week assessment depending on surface area. You get a written report, a walkthrough with your engineers, and a retest window.
